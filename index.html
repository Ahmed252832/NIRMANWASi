Core capabilities

One system, four focused workspaces.

Each role follows the same underlying project information while receiving a clear, relevant set of actions.

01 / ADMIN

System oversight

Review people, contractors, properties, operations, finance, complaints, and procurement.

02 / EMPLOYEE

Operational action

Publish tenders, confirm allocations, verify payments, resolve issues, and monitor work.

03 / CLIENT

Property journey

Move clearly from projects and units to bookings, allocations, payments, and support.

04 / REPRESENTATIVE

Bid and progress

Review tenders, submit bids, follow awards, and provide construction progress updates.
